What Sets Me Apart

Economics, sales,
and then design.

What sets me apart is a diversified background in economics and sales. I don't just understand how to build great experiences — I understand how to articulate their business value. I'm as comfortable in a boardroom discussing ROI as I am in a design review discussing hierarchy and interaction patterns. That fluency makes me effective at aligning stakeholders, securing buy-in, and ensuring design decisions are made with business context — not just defended from outside it.

My foundation is classic double-diamond thinking, but my application has evolved — I focus less on personal craft and more on building systems, processes, and teams that consistently deliver user-centered outcomes. Before design, I was in sales advocating for technology adoption. My job wasn't pushing products — it was making them indispensable. That mindset still drives me.
